> "Ek pizza ke paise kyun de rahe ho, jab 2499 mein do large pizza mil rahe hain, Tuesday tak?"

**B: The Mistake, 58/100 WORKABLE**
> "Ek pizza order karna band karo. 2499 mein do large pizza, Tuesday tak."
> (Stop ordering one pizza. You get two large ones for 2499 rupees until Tuesday.)

Both scored weakest on STAKES (what it costs to keep ordering the old way). That's fine for a
deal Short: the cost is the second pizza they miss out on. Don't add a "you save Rs X" line
unless you know the exact normal price.
